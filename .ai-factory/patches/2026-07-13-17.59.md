# Restrict manual gift recipients to finishers and DNF participants

**Date:** 2026-07-13 17:59
**Files:** backend/internal/domain/entity/participant.go, backend/internal/application/query/get_miniapp_participants.go, backend/internal/application/command/set_manual_gift_recipient.go, backend/internal/application/command/update_gift.go, backend/internal/infrastructure/http/handler/miniapp.go, backend/internal/infrastructure/http/handler/gifts.go, frontend/src/utils/miniappMyGifts.ts
**Severity:** high

## Problem

The Mini App recipient picker returned every event participant. Registered
participants who had not started (DNS) and disqualified participants could be
selected manually or receive a random manual gift when they had no prize.

## Root Cause

The recipient-options query transformed every participant from `FindByEvent`
without applying ride-status eligibility. Random assignment consumed that same
unfiltered list, while both Mini App and administrator write paths validated
only that a recipient belonged to the event.

## Solution

Define the eligibility rule in the domain: a manual recipient must either have
a current result or be marked DNF, and must not be disqualified. Filter the
Mini App options with that rule, validate it again in both manual-assignment
commands, and expose the rejected write as an HTTP conflict. Add structured
`[FIX:manual-recipient-eligibility]` logs and update the Mini App error text.

## Prevention

- Keep recipient eligibility as a domain predicate and reuse it in read and
  write paths.
- Treat filtered UI lists as convenience only; enforce the same rule at every
  mutation boundary.
- Cover DNS, DNF, finished, and disqualified recipients in list, random, and
  direct-assignment tests.
